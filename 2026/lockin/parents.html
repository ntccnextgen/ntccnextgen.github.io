Parents & Leaders

Preparation, participation, registration, and safety

Parent and participant information

What to bring

  • Paper Bible , sleeping bag/blanket and pillow, modest comfortable clothing, sleepwear, toiletries, and personal medication according to church policy.
  • A completed permission and conduct agreement, medical release, emergency contacts, and information about dietary or medical needs.
  • A willing attitude to participate, include others, and respect quiet time.

Technology and phones

The theme UNPLUGGED encourages voluntary device-free activity blocks rather than an unsafe blanket ban. Phones may be stored during games and worship with an agreed, supervised process; students must still have a way to contact a parent or guardian, and leaders retain emergency communication access. Obtain guardian consent before photographing or publishing identifiable images.

Safety and adult-leader plan

WarningEssential safeguarding conditions

Do not run an overnight youth event unless church leadership approves the safeguarding plan and sufficient screened adults are available to supervise every active and sleeping area. Two adults should never be interpreted as sufficient for every group size or building layout; staffing must follow church policy, applicable requirements, and the number and age of participants.

  • Supervision: Screen and train leaders; apply a two-adult/observable-interaction policy, with gender-appropriate supervision and no isolated one-to-one encounters.
  • Facility: Define approved rooms; secure off-limits spaces; check exits, accessibility, lighting, alarms, and outdoor-door controls. No dark or confined-space games.
  • Attendance: Maintain check-in/check-out records, parent-authorized pickup details, adult zone assignments, and regular student counts.
  • Rest: Provide designated, separated sleeping areas in accordance with church policies, sufficient supervision, and a genuinely quiet overnight period.
  • Medical: Review allergies, medications, dietary needs, emergency contacts, first aid readiness, and emergency response procedures before the event.
  • Conduct: No bullying, exclusion, hazing, rough physical play, inappropriate content, or unauthorized departure. Respect personal boundaries and privacy.
  • Food: Follow food-allergy procedures, food safety practices, and suitable dietary accommodations.
  • Reporting: Clearly identify a safeguarding contact, follow incident-reporting requirements, and inform parents promptly when warranted.
  • Cancellation: Postpone or adapt to an evening-only program if safe overnight staffing and facilities cannot be provided.
